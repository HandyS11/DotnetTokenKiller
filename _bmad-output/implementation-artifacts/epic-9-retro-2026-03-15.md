## Key Insights

1. **Cleanup sprints succeed when the decision behind them was sound** — Epic 8's scope analysis was correct; Epic 9 just executed it. The hard work was the analysis, not the deletion.
2. **Low-urgency action items drift without structural enforcement** — soft carry-forwards don't work. They need AC-level gates in story files to have real accountability.
3. **Batch execution constraints should be visible in story ordering** — not just buried in dev notes. Sprint-status or story headers should flag when stories must be executed as a batch.
4. **Pre-release is the right moment for breaking changes** — the tool's identity (4-command MSBuild noise reducer) is now explicit and defensible before any public release.
