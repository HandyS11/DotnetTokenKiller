## Key Insights

1. **First story into a new layer pays the incident tax** — expect it, document the patterns, and move on; trying to pre-solve it via prep tasks has limited return
2. **Forward-looking implementation is a force multiplier** — Story 5.1 dev pre-completing 5.2's tracking block eliminated a full story of implementation work; this approach should be recognised and encouraged
3. **Hard gates kill zombies; loose assignments don't** — the Epic 4 commitment to hard gates (not "alongside Story X") worked on the first attempt
4. **Story notes are sufficient for pitfall capture** — MEMORY.md should be reserved for cross-epic patterns; per-story pitfalls are findable in the story record
